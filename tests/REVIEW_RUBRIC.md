# HUMAN REVIEW RUBRIC v1

Use observed evidence only. Scores assist review; they do not automatically equal VERIFIED.

Score 1–5:
- Semantic alignment
- Visual distinctness from control
- Repeatability
- Specificity
- Side-effect control
- Cross-context robustness
- Production usefulness

## Decision guidance
VERIFIED: clear intended mechanism, useful repeatability, defensible evidence.
CONTEXTUAL: works materially only in stated contexts/subjects/models.
UNSTABLE: intended concept appears but repeatability/control is weak.
REJECTED: effect cannot be defensibly isolated, is misleading, or adds no useful distinction.
UNTESTED: protocol incomplete.

## Mandatory notes
Reviewer must state:
1. what visibly changed;
2. what unexpectedly changed;
3. whether the slash itself is the likely cause;
4. limitations;
5. why the final status was chosen.

No automatic verification from numeric score alone.
