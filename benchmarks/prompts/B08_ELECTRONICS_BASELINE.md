# B08 — Consumer Electronics Baseline

Status: DRAFT — reference asset not yet approved.

## Purpose
Primary technical-product benchmark for exploded/cutaway/assembly tests.

## Requirement
Use a reference product whose visible and internal component structure is known well enough to review. Prefer a deliberately simple project-owned mock product or a documented generic object rather than inventing internals for a proprietary device.

## Baseline
Neutral orthographic-style product presentation, plain background, even illumination, no exploded separation, no technical callouts.

## Lock
Exterior geometry and all known component relationships. Record which internals are known before testing.
