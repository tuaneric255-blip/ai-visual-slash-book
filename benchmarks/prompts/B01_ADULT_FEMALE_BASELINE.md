# B01 — Adult Female Portrait Baseline

Status: DRAFT — reference image not yet approved.

## Purpose
Neutral benchmark for pose, camera, composition and lighting tests.

## Baseline prompt
Photorealistic full-body studio photograph of the same adult female reference subject. Neutral relaxed standing posture, arms resting naturally, feet fully visible, camera at eye level, straight-on view, neutral perspective, even soft neutral illumination, plain light-gray seamless studio background, contemporary plain crew-neck top, straight-leg trousers and simple sneakers. Natural body proportions and realistic skin texture. No dramatic styling, no cinematic effects, no exaggerated pose.

## Lock
Identity/reference subject; age appearance; hairstyle; outfit; background; aspect ratio; baseline framing; baseline lighting except when lighting is the tested variable.

## Variable overrides
Camera tests may override viewpoint only.
Pose tests may override body pose only.
Composition tests may override spatial organization/framing only.
Lighting tests may override lighting only.

## Negative controls
Do not add beauty, fashion-editorial, cinematic, golden-hour, lens, mood or location modifiers unless they are explicitly under test.
