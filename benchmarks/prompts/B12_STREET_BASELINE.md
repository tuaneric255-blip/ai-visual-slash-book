## Purpose
Composition, perspective, camera and city/environment tests.

## Baseline prompt
Photorealistic contemporary urban pedestrian street with a single adult benchmark subject standing naturally near the middle distance. Neutral daylight, no dramatic weather, no intentional leading-line instruction, no symmetry instruction, no cinematic grading. Preserve the same street identity, subject identity, wardrobe and time baseline across an A/B series.

## Warning
Real streets inherently contain lines and perspective. Review must compare whether the tested slash reorganizes visual attention rather than merely detecting that lines exist.
