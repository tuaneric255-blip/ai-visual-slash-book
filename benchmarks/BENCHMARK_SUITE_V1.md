# BENCHMARK SUITE v1

Purpose: fixed test subjects for controlled A/B slash experiments.

## B01 — Adult Female Portrait
Use: pose, camera, composition, lighting, fashion, city.
Baseline: adult woman, neutral contemporary outfit, simple studio/neutral street as required, natural proportions.
Lock: identity/reference when supported, age appearance, basic hair, outfit for same test series, environment, aspect ratio.
Do not lock when it is the tested variable.

## B02 — Adult Male Portrait
Same control principles as B01.

## B03 — Couple
Use: interaction, composition, camera, lighting.
Lock: identities, wardrobe, relative location unless tested.

## B04 — Group of Four
Use: group arrangement, hierarchy, team pose, composition.

## B05 — Perfume Bottle
Use: product camera, composition, lighting, FX, technical.
Lock: exact bottle geometry, cap, liquid, label/logo, typography and materials.

## B06 — Skincare Bottle
Use: packaging/material/background/beauty product.

## B07 — Beverage Can/Bottle
Use: condensation, splash, cold/fresh cues, advertising.

## B08 — Consumer Electronics
Use: material, edge light, technical/exploded/hero views.

## B09 — Fashion Item
Use: lay-flat, ecommerce, material, bundle/presentation.

## B10 — Food Dish
Use: camera, composition, light, steam/food advertising.

## B11 — Interior
Use: perspective, composition, lighting.

## B12 — Architecture/Street
Use: leading lines, symmetry, perspective, weather/city.

## Benchmark approval gate
A benchmark becomes ACTIVE only after:
- reference asset exists;
- baseline prompt exists;
- lock variables documented;
- known limitations documented;
- human approval recorded.

Until then: DRAFT.
