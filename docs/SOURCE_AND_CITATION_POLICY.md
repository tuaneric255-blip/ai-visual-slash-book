# SOURCE & CITATION POLICY

## Evidence layers
A. Professional visual definition — credible photography/design/visual communication sources.
B. Platform behavior — official platform/model documentation where available.
C. Project observation — recorded test assets and human review.
D. Author convention — explicitly labeled project-defined slash/macro.

Never use layer D as evidence for A–C.

## Research requirements
Before publishing specialist entries such as Rembrandt lighting, contrapposto, golden ratio, lens terminology, Gestalt or engineering visualization, record credible source(s) in source_registry.csv.

## Web content
Paraphrase and cite. Do not copy long passages. Preserve source title, organization/author, URL and access date.

## Platform freshness
Platform-specific behavior can change. Record test date and model/version where available. Avoid wording that implies permanence.

## Images
Use project-generated benchmark/test imagery or properly licensed/source-cleared material. Test evidence originals must remain unretouched with respect to the variable being evaluated.
