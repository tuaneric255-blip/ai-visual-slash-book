## 48.2. Cold Beverage

## 48.3. Skincare KOC

## 48.4. E-commerce Bundle
SKU locks → `/bundlepack` → `/purewhite` → consistent scale → contact shadows.

## 48.5. Technical Explainer

## 48.6. Social Campaign
Hero subject/product → visual intent → composition → copy safe area → brand palette → platform crop QA.
