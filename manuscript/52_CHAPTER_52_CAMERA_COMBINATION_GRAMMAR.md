# CHƯƠNG 52 — CAMERA COMBINATION GRAMMAR

## 52.1. Bốn lớp camera
**Elevation/Viewpoint + Orientation + Framing + Perspective/Optics**

Ví dụ:
`/lowangle /threequarterview /fullbody /35mm`

## 52.2. Viewpoint primitives
`/eyelevel` · `/highangle` · `/lowangle` · `/topdown` · `/birdseye` · `/wormseye` · `/groundlevel`.

## 52.3. Orientation
`/frontview` · `/profile` · `/threequarterview` · `/backview` · `/overtheshoulder`.

## 52.4. Framing
`/extremecloseup` · `/closeup` · `/mediumshot` · `/kneeshot` · `/fullbody` · `/wideshot`.

## 52.5. Perspective modifiers
`/foreshortening` · `/forcedperspective` · `/wideangle` · `/fisheye`.

## 52.6. Distinguishing aerial terms
- `/highangle`: camera simply above subject.
- `/topdown`: camera points near vertically downward.
- `/birdseye`: very high aerial viewpoint.
- `/droneview`: broader aerial/drone-photography aesthetic; composite candidate.

These are not automatically synonyms.

## 52.7. Combination examples
`/stand /stepforward /wormseye /fullbody /foreshortening`
`/sit /kneehug /birdseye /wideshot`
`/lookback /overtheshoulder /closeup`

### Tóm tắt chương
Camera combination works best when viewpoint, orientation, framing and perspective are explicitly separated.
